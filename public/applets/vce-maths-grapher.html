Axes & window

Use ^ for indices: x^2 → x2. Braces for longer: 10^{-2}. Subscripts with _, e.g. x_{1}.

Grid & numbering

With π numbering on, the step is in units of π — e.g. step 0.5 gives gridlines and labels every π/2.

Curves0

Equations y(x) with a restricted domain, points lists, or parametrics x(t), y(t) for circles and ellipses. Eye = show/hide. ANS = answer-only: hidden in the Student view.

Intercepts

Finds and labels the x- and y-intercepts of every visible equation or points curve, only where they fall inside the window. Exact mode recognises fractions, multiples of π and surds, e.g. (1 + √5)/2.

Intersections0

Marks and labels every point of intersection between the two chosen curves that falls inside the window. Uses the Intercepts value format (exact / decimal).

Asymptotes0

Dashed vertical or horizontal lines with an automatic x = a / y = b label (or your own). Oblique asymptotes: add a dashed equation curve instead.

Tangent lines0

Draws the tangent to a curve at x₀ (gradient found numerically), full width of the window.

Guide lines0

Dashed construction lines from a point to the axes. Snap to a curve to compute y from its equation.

Shaded areas0

Shades a region over an x-interval: between a curve and the x axis, above or below a curve, or between two curves.

Points & labels0
Text annotations0

Position is in graph units. Supports ^/_ indices, e.g. a = 2 m s^-2.
