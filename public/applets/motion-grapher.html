Freehand curves are lightly smoothed before differentiating, so derived graphs stay readable. Drawn-graph scales are fixed; derived graphs rescale to fit. Shape tools: drag to place, then adjust the handles (hollow = control points); click elsewhere to start the next shape, Esc cancels the current one.
