Transitions

Ironman transitions include long runs through change tents; 4-6 minutes each is normal. Short-course races are much quicker. Defaults update when you switch race.

Nutrition

Typical race targets: 80-120 g/h carbs on the bike (gut-trained), 50-70 g/h on the run, 500-1000 mL/h fluid and 500-1200 mg/h sodium depending on heat and sweat rate. Practise the full plan in training. Not medical advice.
