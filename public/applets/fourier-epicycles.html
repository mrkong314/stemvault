Any closed curve Z(t) equals Σ cₙ·ei(nt+φₙ) — a stack of circles, each spinning at an integer frequency n, with radius |cₙ| and starting phase φₙ set by the discrete Fourier transform of the path. The tip of the last circle traces the shape. Few circles → a smooth blur; sharp corners need many (notice the ripple — that's the Gibbs phenomenon).
