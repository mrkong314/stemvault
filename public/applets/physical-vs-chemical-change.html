Physical or Chemical Change?

Decide whether each change makes a new substance (chemical) or not (physical)
Question 1 of 24 · Score 0
Signs a chemical change has happened (a new substance has formed): a permanent colour change, a gas is produced (fizzing or bubbling), a solid precipitate forms in a solution, energy is released or absorbed (heat, light, sound), an odour is produced, and the change is difficult or impossible to reverse.
Physical changes alter the state, shape or size of a substance, but the particles themselves stay the same; no new substance forms, and the change is usually easy to reverse.
