How to play

One student is the clue-giver and faces the screen. Their team faces away. The clue-giver describes the term without saying it, any part of it, or any of the banned words (plurals and other forms count too). The other teams watch the screen and call "taboo" if a banned word slips out.

Correct guess: +1, next card. Taboo: penalty, next card. Pass: no score, next card, limited per turn. The turn ends when the timer runs out.

Keyboard: G or → got it, P or ↓ pass, T or ← taboo, Space start or pause the turn, Enter next team, F fullscreen.
