An interactive tool for exploring and sampling the BGS brand colour palette. Features all 15 official colours including BGS Blue, red variants, and house colours (Armstrong, Crowther, Dixon, Fairweather, Hancock, Melville, Rofe, School Red), plus white, black, and placeholder grey.

The picker displays a live preview with automatic WCAG contrast ratio calculation against white backgrounds, helping ensure colour choices maintain accessibility standards. Copy hex codes to clipboard with one click.
